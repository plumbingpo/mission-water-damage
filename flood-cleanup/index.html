Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Day or night, 24/7, Mission Restoration Co sends flood cleanup crews throughout Mission to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Mission

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
