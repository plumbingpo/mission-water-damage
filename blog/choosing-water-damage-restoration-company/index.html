Selecting a Restoration Professional in Mission, TX

Home / Blog / Selecting a Restoration Professional in Mission, TX

Picking a restoration company is a major decision for any Mission, TX owner dealing with water damage. The company you choose will determine how quickly your property is restored, how thoroughly the damage is addressed, how much you'll pay out of pocket, and whether you'll face secondary problems like mold growth months later. Not every outfit is qualified, and hiring the wrong one can mean inflated invoices, a job left incomplete, or something worse. Use the points below to tell a strong restoration company apart from a weak one.

Confirm the Crew Is IICRC Certified

Restoration credentials are measured against the IICRC, short for the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification. IICRC-certified technicians have completed rigorous training in water damage restoration science, including proper extraction techniques, structural drying methodology, moisture measurement, and mold prevention. Ask any restoration company you're considering whether their technicians hold current IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD). If a company cannot prove those credentials, move on.

Ask for Proof of Insurance and Licenses

Every restoration company working on your property should carry comprehensive insurance including general liability and workers' compensation. Ask to see the insurance certificates, then check that they have not expired. If the contractor is underinsured, an injury or further damage during the job could become your liability. Check as well that it has every business license Mission or Texas rules call for.

Check Availability and Arrival Speed

Water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. Real 24/7 emergency service, with crews that reach your neighborhood fast, should be part of the deal. Ask specifically about their response time to Mission and confirm that their emergency line is answered by a live person, not a voicemail or answering service that adds delay.

Ask About Equipment and Process

Professional restoration requires professional equipment. Ask what type of extraction equipment the company uses (truck-mounted units are superior to portable-only operations), what type of dehumidifiers they deploy (LGR units are the industry standard), and whether they use thermal imaging for moisture detection. Also ask about their drying monitoring process: reputable companies monitor moisture levels daily and adjust equipment based on readings, not just place equipment and hope for the best.

Check Reviews and References

A company's history shows up clearly in its online reviews. Search Yelp, Google, and the BBB to see if customer satisfaction, professional conduct, and work quality hold up consistently. Pay particular attention to reviews that mention the company's communication, timeliness, insurance assistance, and the final quality of restoration work. Don't hesitate to ask for references from recent water damage projects in Mission.

Get Clear on Prices and Insurance Billing

Reputable restoration companies provide detailed estimates before beginning work and can explain their pricing structure clearly. Xactimate and similar industry-standard pricing programs, recognized and accepted by insurance carriers, are used by many quality contractors. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. Companies that demand large upfront payments or refuse to work with insurance should raise concerns.
